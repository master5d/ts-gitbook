🗺️ Roadmap: Vibe Coder / AI Generalist

9 core modules + 3 optional. 58 units total: 43 in the core and 15 in optional tracks.

🎯 Overview

An AI Generalist is a full-stack problem solver who assembles solutions with AI. The course is agent-agnostic: skills transfer between Claude Code, Codex, Hermes, goose, OpenCode, nanobot, and CheetahClaws — including on a sovereign stack.

🪜 Course modules

Each module follows Kolb’s cycle: Activation → Reflection → Concept → Practice. The core is 00–08; 09–11 are optional.

🧭 The next step: AI Solution Architect

The course leads to designing an agentic system around a real problem: setting AI boundaries, splitting work into AI / Tool / Code, and choosing the stack, memory, observability, and fallback route. It does not promise a job or income.
